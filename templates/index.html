Who Needs JobShield AI?
  • College Students: Evaluating internship letters received via LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or job boards.
  • Fresh Graduates: Checking entry-level offers with high upfront promises.
  • Campus Placement Cells: Screening unsolicited recruiter notifications sent to students.
  • Student Clubs & Mentors: Guiding peers on proper due diligence before signing contracts.
  • The Need: A quick, understandable first-check that helps students pause, investigate, and make informed choices.
Detection Capabilities

What JobShield AI Scans For

Our dual Rules + NLP inspection engine cross-examines offers against the top recruitment risk vectors.

Upfront Fee Demands

Flags registration fees, training bond costs, kit deposits, and direct UPI or QR transfer instructions.

Unverified Contacts

Detects free webmail accounts (@gmail/@yahoo) pretending to be enterprise HR and unofficial Telegram/WhatsApp channels.

Unrealistic Promises

Identifies "Direct Selection without Interview", disproportionate pay for trivial tasks, and guaranteed payouts.

High-Pressure Tactics

Catches artificial urgency, 24-hour expiration threats, and scarcity coercion designed to prevent second opinions.
