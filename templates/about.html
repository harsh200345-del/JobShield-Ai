JobShield AI

Smart Internship & Job Risk Assessment Platform

"Flags warning signs and explains what to verify — never claims a final fraud verdict."

Harsh Pandey Shivam Yadav Mahi Garg Priyanshi Bansal

Proposed Solution: 4-Step Assessment Workflow

01
SUBMIT

Paste raw job text from messaging apps or upload an offer letter PDF / document.

02
EXTRACT

PyMuPDF & NLP parser read role, employer, pay/stipend, fees, domains, and contacts.

03
ANALYZE

Dual Rules + NLP engine evaluates 5 safety vectors to identify suspicious indicators.

04
EXPLAIN

Risk score, transparent reasons, radar visualization, and actionable checklist.

Technology Stack & System Architecture

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5
  • Backend: Python 3 + Flask Application Framework
  • AI / NLP: Rule-Based Risk Engine + NLP Heuristics / LLM API
  • Documents: PyMuPDF (fitz) + PyPDF fallback
  • Database: SQLite (jobshield.db)
  • Visualization: Chart.js (Radar & Score Gauges)
High-Level Data Flow
USER INPUT (PDF / Pasted text)
    ↓
FLASK BACKEND (Input validation & PyMuPDF extract)
    ↓
ANALYSIS ENGINE (5-Category Rules + NLP reasoner)
    ↓
RESULTS PAGE (Risk indicators, radar & checklist)
MVP Privacy Principle: Process documents in-memory for risk analysis and avoid retaining personal candidate data unless needed and consented to.

Impact & Responsible AI Principles

Student Awareness

Helps applicants notice warning signs before sharing money or sensitive documentation like Aadhaar, PAN, or bank credentials.

Transparent Explainability

Explains the specific text evidence that triggered each warning rather than giving an unexplained, opaque score label.

Campus Adoption

Can be integrated into college placement cells, WhatsApp career groups, and student guidance clubs to protect entire batches.

Strategic Product Roadmap

Phase 1: MVP (Complete)
Input, Extraction & Warning Engine

Text paste, PyMuPDF PDF extraction, 5-vector rule engine, Chart.js radar visualization, and SQLite history database.

Phase 2: BETA (Active)
AI Explanations & Report Archive

Contextual NLP explanations, student feedback loop, pre-loaded benchmark test suites, and interactive checklist persistence.

Phase 3: Campus Pilot
Placement-Cell Rollout

Live MCA company database API lookups, placement cell supervisor alerts, and university-wide recruiter whitelisting.
